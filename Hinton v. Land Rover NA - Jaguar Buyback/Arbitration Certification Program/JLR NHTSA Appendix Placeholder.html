Executive Summary

Analysis of 3,401 NHTSA complaints for Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) vehicles reveals systemic issues, with 884 electrical, 165 cooling, 1,101 warranty, and 2,234 repair-related complaints. The Jaguar XF (511) and Range Rover Sport (455) lead in volume. Overlap across themes suggests persistent defects, with 4,384 total flags exceeding the 3,401 rows. Narratives highlight recurring electrical failures, coolant leaks, and warranty disputes, mirroring the 2020 Jaguar F-Type case (JAG2501941). These patterns underscore potential deficiencies in JLR’s warranty and repair processes, relevant to the BBB AUTO LINE review under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act.
