Description of the Noncompliance : A concern has been identified on certain 2019 and 2020 Model Year Jaguar F- TYPE vehicles fitted with 18 inch rear tires, where the tire placard label located on the driver’s door opening incorrectly states the wrong rear tire size for the vehicle.

Vehicles where the label does not have the correct rear tire size stated on the tire placard label will not comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) 110 - Tire Selection and Rims for Passenger Cars.

Description of Noncompliance :

FMVSS 1 : 110 - Tire selection and rims FMVSS 2 : NR

Description of the Safety Risk : Where the tire placard label states the incorrect tire size, the customer may fit a tire with dimensions that are not to the as designed intent which may cause a change in the vehicle handling characteristics and increase the risk of a crash. Jaguar Land Rover first identified a concern with the tire placard label during a Government Regulations Coordinator (GRC) vehicle audit event at the Castle Bromwich vehicle assembly plant. Following plant investigations, a regulatory Stop Shipment was implemented on August 29, 2019.

On September 03, 2019 the Jaguar Land Rover Product Safety and Compliance Committee (PSCC) opened an investigation to review the concern. Engineering investigated the concern and confirmed the error on the tire placard for the rear tire size originated in the engineering design release phase. On September 17, 2019 the PSCC agreed that the matter be progressed to the Recall Determination Committee (RDC).

On September 19, 2019, the RDC determined that the condition represented a compliance concern and affected vehicles do not fully meet the requirements of FMVSS 110 Tire Selection and Rims for Passenger Cars. A non- compliance recall will be conducted to correct this error.

There have been no reports of accidents or injuries as a result of this concern.

Chronology :

Description of Remedy Program : Owners will be notified and instructed to take their vehicle to a Jaguar authorized repairer, who will attach a tire placard label that states the correct rear tire size.

There will be no charge to owners for this repair.
