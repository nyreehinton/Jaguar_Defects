To be eligible, your vehicle must fall under the Song Beverly Act.  Section 1793.22 of the Song Beverly Act states that a “New motor vehicle” means a “new motor vehicle” (including the chassis cab of a motor home, a dealer-owned vehicle, a “demonstrator,” or other vehicle sold with a manufacturer’s new car warranty.)

Per Rodriguez v. FCA US, LLC, S274625 and the Supreme Court of California’s decision regarding the interpretation of the phrase “other motor vehicle sold with a manufacturer’s new car warranty”, your vehicle would not be eligible as it is did not have the manufacturer’s new vehicle warranty issued at the time of sale or lease.

The  Supreme Court of California has concluded that a motor vehicle purchased with an unexpired manufacturer’s new car warranty does not qualify as a ‘motor vehicle sold with a manufacturer’s new car warranty’ under section 1793.22 of the Song Beverly Act unless the new car warranty was issued with the sale.
