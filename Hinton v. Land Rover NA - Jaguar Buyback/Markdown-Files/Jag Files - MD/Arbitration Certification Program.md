WHAT IS ARBITRATION?

Tel: (916) 574-7350 Fax: (916) 574-8638 Email: acp@dca.ca.gov

Arbitration is a free and relatively simple way of resolving warranty disputes between manufacturers and consumers. Both you and the manufacturer agree to allow a neutral third party (an arbitrator) to decide whether a reasonable number of repair attempts have been made and what relief could be granted.
