Ms. Sorensen was appointed as a non‑executive director of JLR Automotive PLC in August 2018. Ms Sorensen was then also appointed to the Board of Directors of Jaguar Land Rover Holdings Limited and Jaguar Land Rover Limited in July 2019.  She is a Non‑Executive Director of both companies. Ms. Sorensen holds an MSc in Economics and Management from the University of Aarhus and is on the boards and committees of various international companies, including Tata Consultancy Services, Tata Motors and Sulzer.
