NIGEL BLENKINSOP | JLR Media Newsroom

Nigel was appointed Executive Director, Industrial Operations in February 2025, and has accountability for the complete ecosystem of how JLR delivers vehicles to its global markets. A key priority for Nigel will be ensuring JLR’s robust, sustainable end‑to‑end supply chain works as part of a fully deployed and connected industrial strategy. This aims to deliver greater efficiency and resilience of the company’s industrial operations.

He began his career at Ford Motor Company, holding several positions during a 14‑year stay, before moving to BAE Systems as a Director in 2005, where he spent seven years.

Nigel joined JLR in 2012 to lead the creation of our Engine Manufacturing Centres in the UK, China and India, before heading up our Solihull Operations and then taking up an Executive Director position leading the Quality function. Until recently, Nigel led the core transformation functions, bringing alignment and value‑creation focus to Information & Digital Technology, Business Excellence, Technology Business Services India and the Value Optimisation Office. He has been instrumental in our Reimagine strategy and has proved to be the type of leader who truly acts for the enterprise, end to end.

Nigel is a strong team leader with a passion for developing the next generation of talent. He is at the forefront of the sector, and is a member of several external bodies, including the Shingo Executive Advisory Board, the Institute of Engineering Technology, and chairs the Tata Network Forum for Business Excellence in Europe.
