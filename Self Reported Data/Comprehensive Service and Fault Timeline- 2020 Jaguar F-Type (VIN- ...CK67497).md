## **Event 1: Initial Electrical Failure**

- **Date:** October 1 - 2, 2024
- **Mileage:** 23,072
- **Service Facility:** Jaguar Land Rover South Bay
- **Customer Complaint:**

- **Technician's Diagnostic Findings:**

- **Service Actions Performed:**
  - Repaired the ground wire.
  - Replaced the blown fuse.
  - Verified power was restored to the left front door module and all associated switches were functioning.

## **Event 2: Recurring Electrical Failure and Subsequent Battery Failure**

- **Date:** October 7 - 10, 2024
- **Mileage:** 23,278
- **Service Facility:** Jaguar Land Rover Los Angeles
- **Customer Complaints:**
  - "Client states that the drivers side door electronics do not operate."
  - "Client states low battery warning light comes on the cluster."
- **Technician's Diagnostic Findings:**
  - **Electrical:** Attempted to connect diagnostic tool (TOPIX), but no connection was possible. Found fuse RF 08 in the rear junction box was blown again. After replacing the fuse and driving two miles, it blew again. A detailed circuit trace was performed, which included:
    - Checking harness resistance values from the door module, diagnostic connector, and between the engine and rear junction boxes.
    - A circuit was found **"shorted to ground where driver rests his/her left foot underneath the carpet."**
  - **Battery:** Checked for codes and found **DTC U3003-16 (Battery Voltage)** stored in the Gear Shift Module (GSM). Performed an in-vehicle battery test, which **FAILED**. Recharged the battery and performed a second in-vehicle test, which also **FAILED**. Performed an out-of-vehicle battery test, which **FAILED**.
- **Service Actions Performed:**
  - Performed harness repair on the circuit from connector C13H-13 to C1BB01A-15 to resolve the short to ground.
  - Replaced the main start-up battery (Part #T2H33020).
  - Reset the Battery Monitoring System (BMS).
  - Cleared all codes and verified door electronics were working correctly.
- **Costs:**
  - **Repair Cost:** $0.00 (Likely performed under warranty or goodwill)
  - **Total Invoice Amount:** $0.00
