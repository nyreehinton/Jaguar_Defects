# Communications Related to Powertrain System Faults

The diagnostic analysis of the 2020 Jaguar F-Type (VIN SAJDD1GX4LCK67497) revealed complex issues ranging from fundamental network integrity faults to specific powertrain and body control concerns.

_The communications issued by Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) related to the specific faults identified in the diagnostic report and Mode 6 analysis are listed below:_

## Communications Related to Powertrain System Fault

This addresses the **VVT Monitor Failure** (Mode 6 MID 35) and the **Coolant System History Codes** (`P2B61-73`, `P26CB-72`). These issues are related to the Ingenium I4 2.0L Petrol engine (AJ20P).

The diagnostic report highlighted **critical permanent faults** in the Gateway Module (GWM) (`B1412-96`) and Passenger Seat Module (PSM) (`U3001-46`), alongside **widespread CAN/LIN bus errors**. These suggest a systemic electrical problem or failure in the central communication hub.
